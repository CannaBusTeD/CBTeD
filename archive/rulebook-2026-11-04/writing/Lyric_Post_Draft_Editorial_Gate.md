# CBTeD Lyric Post-Draft Editorial Gate

Status: Mandatory Writing Studio operating procedure for CBTeD-authored **new song lyrics**, especially when a song and thumbnail/cover are requested together.
Authority: The full universal master workflow governs HOW every separate action is performed. This file is a genre-specific workflow beneath the master; it does not change the universal rules or replace an existing authorised lyric master, project rules, corrections or approved source.

## Why the gate exists

An initial plausible lyric is a **draft, not a finished song**. The assistant must not confuse "I have enough of a concept to make a thumbnail" with "the lyrics have passed editorial inspection". Perform the three editorial stages as real, distinct operations, with a source check and outcome check for each.

## Order for a NEW song + thumbnail request

1. **Retrieve / load rules**. Consult the full universal rules, Writing Studio procedure, all applicable lyric and project rules, prior approved material and character references. Establish the title, intended meaning, emotional effect, authorial voice, form, and prohibitions.
2. **Write draft 1**. Compose a complete original lyric with a strong narrative concept, effective hook, realistic speech/singing cadence and purposefully developed sections. Mark it **unreviewed working draft**, not final or approved. Preserve a complete unchanged copy for comparison.
3. **Commission the thumbnail from the established concept**. When the song's central concept, imagery, title and artist wording are stable enough, read the Visual Studio and image-generation rules, prepare one standalone thumbnail prompt and submit exactly one requested thumbnail generation through an actually available ChatGPT image tool. Record returned task/job status. Do not claim completion merely because generation has started. The image should represent the durable whole-song concept, rather than hinge on a weak lyric line likely to be revised. If no thumbnail is requested, omit this step without changing the editorial stages.
4. **EDITORIAL STAGE 1: BRUTAL CRITIQUE / AI-SLOP AUDIT**. Switch explicitly from writer to critical editor. Return to draft 1, reread it **in full**, line by line, using the active lyric-specific rules, project prohibitions and real source. Quote the *actual* offending lines internally or in a requested critique and diagnose the exact fault and its effect. Actively hunt for:
   - generic AI cadence, symmetric contrast, repeated parallel constructions and mechanically repeated sentence openings;
   - rule-of-three/list habits, "no X no Y", "not A not B", forced neatness, vague uplift or false profundity;
   - filler rhymes, convenient line-ending words, near-rhymes that stretch natural speech, clumsy stresses and unsingable breath groups;
   - stock images, stock emotional labels, generic metaphors, obvious explanations of feelings, unnecessary ornamental adjectives, artificial or dated diction, Americanisms inconsistent with the author's voice;
   - hollow hooks, chorus/verse duplication without development, arbitrary structure, over-repeated words, redundant lines, weak narrative escalation, lost point of view and continuity errors;
   - words whose effect depends on reading rather than being sung, or lines that merely sound impressive without a precise meaning.
   Do not invent faults to fill a checklist. Identify what is genuinely strong and protect purposeful oddness, fresh imagery, humour, useful roughness, and the author's specific intent. A "brutal" critique means accurate and exacting, not performatively insulting.
5. **EDITORIAL STAGE 2: RECOMMENDATIONS / REWRITE PLAN**. Based solely on actual findings, specify the most consequential craft intervention, the necessary individual line/section repairs and why each will improve meaning, voice, hook, meter, clarity or narrative progression. Decide explicitly what must be preserved: premise, title, character identity, narrative facts, successful hook/lines, approved lyrics and user negative constraints. Recommendations are a **separate operation**, not silently folded into the critique or rewrite. When the author has authorised a finished new song, the assistant may implement recommendations on its own unapproved draft without asking for another confirmation; recommendations remain proposals until the rewritten text is delivered. Never presume approval to change the user's separately supplied or previously approved lyrics.
6. **EDITORIAL STAGE 3: REWRITE FROM THE SOURCE**. Start from the frozen draft and the recommendations, not from a vague memory of the song. Revise the full newly generated lyric only within the user's authorised brief. Replace genuinely weak material; preserve successful unconventional lines and exact approved material. No new stock metaphors or automatic parallel-pattern substitutions. Maintain believable contemporary British diction, coherent narrative escalation, intentional rhyme/cadence, and a hook whose meaning deepens through the verses. Produce a **distinct revised full draft** and compare it against the baseline and the planned fixes.
7. **Final editorial verification / proofreading**. Independently reread the rewritten full lyric, scan for remaining synthetic patterns, accidental repeats, clumsy stresses, unearned rhymes, continuity problems and violations of the actual writing rules. Proofread mechanical errors *only after* substantive revision. Run a read-aloud/sing-through stress-and-breath check. If a material flaw remains, return to the relevant critique/repair stage for that flaw; do not claim a perfect song or perform cosmetic replacements for their own sake. Make the revised result the **delivery draft**; call it "author-approved" only after the user approves it.
8. **Check thumbnail vs final lyrics**. Inspect the actual image result when available, including text, character identity, aspect ratio, and source alignment. If the rewritten lyric materially changes the concept, investigate whether the existing image still fits; if not, correct the *thumbnail* after the lyric is stable. Preserve a valid generated image when changes are immaterial. If an image job remains pending, disclose that status instead of implying completion.
9. **Deliver**. Present the newly revised full lyric, not unreviewed draft 1, together with the verified thumbnail when completed. If editorial changes are of interest to the user, include a concise note identifying the key weakness removed. Respect requested presentation format and preserve each separately requested creative output. Record draft, critique findings, recommendations, revised version and image/task state when the authenticated project and relevant save tools are actually available; otherwise keep state explicitly in the current conversation.

## Hard separation and edge cases

- **Stage 1 critique, Stage 2 recommendations and Stage 3 rewrite are three distinct stages.** Do not merge or skip them to make a result arrive faster.
- The thumbnail may render while the three editorial stages are executed in ChatGPT. This is *not* an excuse to skip an editorial stage or claim the thumbnail is done; the image job must be genuinely submitted and its result checked separately.
- The workflow is mandatory for **new lyrics CBTeD is asked to write**, whether or not a thumbnail accompanies them. For a user-supplied draft, approved/published lyric, critique-only request, formatting-only request or task that expressly prohibits rewriting, follow that task's authorised scope instead. Do not overwrite already-approved creative work or start an image unless it was requested.
- These are quality checks, not a claim that all patterned writing is inherently bad. Deliberate repetitions, rhymes, motifs, departures from natural speech and surrealism can remain when they do real dramatic or musical work.
- The named lyric-critique example PDF is a prior example, **not** automatically a universal lyric master prompt. Use it as an illustrative reference only when its contents are actually retrieved and relevant.
- Follow the full master workflow's pre-action verification, source fidelity, atomic steps, correction handling and final compliance check before and between all operations.
